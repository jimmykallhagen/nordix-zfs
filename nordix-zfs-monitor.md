# Nordix ZFS Monitor

Widgets for system monitor.
 - zfs-arc-monitor
 - zfs-system-monitor

---

**zfs-arc-monitor**
  - ARC Stat - Shows how much ARC is being utilized and the ARC hitrate

---

**zfs-system-monitor** shows:
  - ZFS Pool Status - How much storage space you have used with a warning if you need to check your snapshots.
  - ARC Stat - Same as the previous monitor, shows how much you are utilizing ARC and ARC hitrate
  - CPU - CPU usage in percentage with CPU temp.
  - RAM - Shows standard overview of RAM memory

---

Both of these monitors are layered in the background and do not interfere with your regular windows.
